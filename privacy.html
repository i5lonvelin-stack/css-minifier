Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Free CSS Minifier ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our CSS minification tool.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information You Provide: Any CSS code you paste into our tool is processed locally in your browser and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics such as the number of minifications performed, but no personal identifying information.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for dark mode preference storage. These cookies are stored only in your browser.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our CSS minification service
  • To understand and analyze how users interact with our service
  • To improve and optimize our website and tool
  • To display relevant advertisements

4. Data Processing

Your CSS code is processed entirely on your browser's client side. We do not transmit, store, or retain your CSS code on our servers. Your privacy is preserved.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for advertising (such as Google AdSense). These services may collect information about your browsing habits to display relevant ads. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of cookies and tracking (except essential ones)
  • Disable ads personalization through Google's Ad Settings

7. Security

We are committed to protecting your information. Since your CSS code is processed locally in your browser, it is not transmitted over the internet to our servers, providing inherent security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@cssminifier.com
